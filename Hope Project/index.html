Clothes for the parts of the day nobody photographs.

Hope makes a small number of pieces, worn on repeat — built from mid-weight cotton and wool that soften with use instead of wearing out. No seasons to chase, just fewer things done properly.

Made in small batches — this season, 6 styles in total.

Fewer, better, longer.

We design around one question: will this still make sense to wear in five years? That rules out a lot. It leaves us with straight seams, natural fibres, and colours pulled from the same quiet palette every year.

Every piece is numbered and logged against the studio it came from, so if a button goes or a hem drops, we can repair it rather than replace it.
