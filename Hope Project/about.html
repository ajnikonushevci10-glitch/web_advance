About the studio

Started at a kitchen table, still small on purpose.

Hope began as a final assignment about slow production. It stayed small because slow production is, on purpose, hard to scale — and that turned out to be the point.

Why "Hope"

The name isn't about optimism as a mood — it's about optimism as a plan. Buying one coat and expecting to still like it in five winters is a small, practical act of hope. We wanted a brand name that held people to that, not just us.

Every fabric choice starts from the same question: does this get better with age, or does it just get older? Boiled wool, brushed cotton, and heavyweight canvas all pass. Most synthetic blends don't, so we don't stock them.

How we got here

  • 2022

    The first batch

    Twelve coats, cut on a kitchen table, sold to classmates and family before the pattern was even graded properly.

  • 2023

    Moved into a shared studio

    Room for a proper cutting table and our first knitting machine, second-hand from a mill that had closed down.

  • 2024

    Started the repair program

    Any Hope piece can be sent back for a free repair, for as long as we're making clothes.

  • 2026

    Six styles, one focused season

    Rather than growing the catalogue, we cut it down to the six pieces we're proudest of.
